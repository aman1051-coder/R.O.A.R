Mentorship

Someone who has walked the path.

Every young radiation oncologist deserves a mentor. ROAR matches residents with senior residents, consultants and alumni who give an hour a month to help with a CV, a fellowship, an exam or a first research project.

How it works

Three steps.

01

Find a mentor

Browse mentors by area (Head & Neck, SBRT, Brachytherapy and more) and by what they help with: CVs, fellowships, exams, research or careers.

02

Send a request

Say where you are in training, what you are aiming for and any deadlines. Up to three requests can wait for an answer at a time.

03

Meet

When a mentor accepts, you both see each other's email and agree how to meet. Mark the request done when you have what you need.

Mentors

Why mentor with ROAR.

  • Give back

    Help the next generation avoid the mistakes you made, an hour a month.

  • On your terms

    Choose the areas and kinds of help you offer, how many mentees you take, and pause at any time.

  • Approved and listed

    The Founders' Council approves every mentor, so members know who they are talking to.

  • Recognised

    Mentors are acknowledged in ROAR's annual report (with their permission).

  • Private

    Contact details are shared only when you accept a request.

  • Open to all stages

    Senior residents can mentor juniors; consultants and alumni can mentor everyone.
