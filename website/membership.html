Your voting window

You can vote and stand for office from 1 year after starting PG until 6 years after completing PG. Taking up a consultant or faculty post does not end your vote; only the 6-year limit does.

How to join

Four steps, all online.

  1. Create an account

    Sign up in the member portal with your email. Until applications open, you can register interest.

  2. Apply online

    Fill in the form and upload your PG college ID card, plus your allotment letter or degree certificate.

  3. Verification

    The Membership Secretary verifies applications within 30 days. Refusals always give reasons, and you can appeal to the Executive Council.

  4. Pay and you're in

    Pay annually or once for life. You get a member number, a digital membership card, and ballots in your portal.

FAQ

Membership questions.

I'm in my first year of PG. Can I join?

Yes. You can join from your first day of PG. Your vote starts 1 year after your PG start date, as shown on your allotment letter.

I've become a consultant. Do I lose my vote?

No. Your vote ends only 6 years after your PG completion date, at the end of that financial year. Your job title doesn't matter.

What happens after 6 years?

You continue as a Senior Member without voting rights. Annual members can keep renewing, and lifetime members pay nothing more. If you hold an office when you pass the limit, you finish that term but can't stand again.

My stipend hasn't been paid. Can I still join?

Yes. If your stipend has been unpaid or delayed for 3 months or more, you can join free on request, with the fee deferred.

I'm a medical oncologist, pathologist or radiologist. Can I join?

Yes, as an Honorary Member, on the recommendation of the President and the Executive Council. Honorary Members don't vote or hold office.

Can I get a refund?

Membership fees are not refundable. Annual membership lapses if it isn't renewed within 3 months of the due date.

When does membership open?

Once ROAR is legally registered and has a bank account in its name. Register your interest and we'll tell you first.
