Privacy note

What we collect. We collect only what you type into our forms. The interest form asks for your name, email, optional WhatsApp number, career stage, institution, State, whether you'd like to be an ambassador, and an optional comment. The contact form asks for your name, email, topic and message.

Why. To reply to you, to contact you about ROAR membership, appoint Institutional Ambassadors, and understand the problems residents face so we can design the national survey.

Who sees it. Only ROAR's founding cabinet. We never sell or share your details. We never name you or your institution in connection with a concern without your written consent.

How long we keep it. Until membership opens and you join or decline, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights. Email us at any time to see, correct or delete your details.
