Our promise

The Member Welfare Charter.

01

Anonymised

Concerns and survey answers are anonymised (Article 8G).

02

Never named without consent

No member or institution is named in connection with a concern without written consent.

03

No exposure

An office-bearer who reveals a reporter's identity is removed from office (Article 8K).

Support desks

Where members can turn.

  • Education Standards Desk

    Confidential help when your training falls short, including teaching and observerships elsewhere.

  • Grievance cell

    Advice on duty hours, delayed stipends, senior-resident pay, contracts, bonds, leave and radiation safety.

  • Dignity at work desk

    Support and guidance on bullying, harassment or discrimination, including POSH and anti-ragging routes.

  • Peer support

    A confidential peer-support network linked to professional mental-health care.

  • Benchmarks

    The national Stipend Index and Senior Resident Pay Benchmark, as they are published.

  • National survey

    Take part in the national survey of radiation oncologists, which is anonymous.

Need help right now?

You don’t need an account for this. Call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416), India’s free 24-hour mental health helpline. For harassment, you can go directly to your institution’s Internal Committee under the POSH Act or its anti-ragging committee.
