Talk to ROAR.

Partnerships, media, events to list, opportunities to post, or a question about joining. A founder will reply.

Raising a grievance?

Please don't send complaints about training, pay or mistreatment through this form. The confidential support desks open after registration, with stronger protection for your identity.

Is ROAR registered?

Registration as a Society in New Delhi is under way. Until it's complete, ROAR doesn't collect fees or open membership.

Does ROAR work with other societies?

Yes. ROAR is independent and seeks to collaborate with national and international radiation oncology societies through MoUs, joint sessions and shared resources. More about partnerships.

Can companies sponsor ROAR?

Only through unrestricted educational grants. Sponsors have no say over content, faculty, elections or advocacy, and every sponsorship is published in the annual report.

How do I become an ambassador?

Tick the ambassador box on the interest form. One resident is appointed in every training institution.
