Rising Oncologists' Alliance in Radiation Oncology · India

India's young radiation oncologists, learning and leading together.

ROAR is the national community of radiation oncology residents and young radiation oncologists in India. We work for excellent training, fair working conditions and wellbeing in every institution, so that every patient is cared for by a confident, well-trained oncologist.

Our mission

A strong next generation for radiation oncology in India.

ROAR brings residents and young radiation oncologists from every State and every training institution together for learning, research, mentorship and mutual support.

We share what works between departments, open doors to modern techniques for every resident, and give young radiation oncologists a collective voice on their training and welfare.

About ROAR and how it is governed →

  • 1
    Unity. One community, with members in every State and every training institution.
  • 2
    Equal exposure. Every resident learns IMRT/VMAT, IGRT, SBRT/SRS, brachytherapy, contouring and planning.
  • 3
    High standards of education. Shared teaching, observerships and open learning resources.
  • 4
    Wellbeing and dignity. Fair hours and pay, safe workplaces, and respect for every member.
Member Welfare Charter

Supporting every member.

Our Member Welfare Charter sets out how ROAR supports residents and young radiation oncologists through evidence, guidance and confidential help.

Working hours

A reasonable working week, rest after long duties, a weekly day off and protected time for study.

Stipend & pay benchmarks

An annual national Stipend Index and Senior Resident Pay Benchmark, published so that every resident can see the picture.

Education Standards Desk

Confidential help to find teaching and observerships when a resident's training needs strengthening.

Dignity at work

Confidential peer support and guidance on the right routes, including POSH committees and anti-ragging mechanisms.

Safety & wellbeing

Radiation safety and dosimetry, and a confidential peer-support network linked to professional mental-health care.

Evidence

National surveys and an annual State of Young Radiation Oncologists in India report.

Confidentiality first. Survey answers and concerns are anonymised, and ROAR never names a member or their institution without the member's written consent. How the support desks will work →
