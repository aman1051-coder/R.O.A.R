Our vision

Training that does not depend on where you were placed.

01

Equal exposure

A minimum exposure standard: the techniques, sites and planning experience every residency should cover, so gaps can be seen and closed.

02

Learning together

Live contouring, case discussions and teaching sessions that connect residents across centres with experienced teachers.

03

A research culture

Multi-centre resident projects with shared authorship, so every resident can take part in research, not only those at large institutes.

Programmes

What we are building for members.

  • ROAR Contour Club

    A monthly live contouring session with expert feedback, with case files to work through afterwards.

  • Learning library

    Guidelines, contouring atlases, courses and exam preparation, chosen by the Academic Society.

  • Members' sessions

    Webinars and Q&As with senior colleagues, with recordings for members who missed them.

  • ROAR Passport

    Structured observerships between Indian centres, so that training does not depend on where you were placed.

  • Research Collective

    Multi-centre resident projects with shared authorship.

  • Minimum exposure standard

    What every residency should cover, and how institutions measure up.
