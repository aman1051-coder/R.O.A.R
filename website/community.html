Inside Community

Made for radiation oncology.

  • Topics

    Head & Neck, Breast, CNS, SBRT & SRS, Brachytherapy, Physics & Planning, Contouring, Exams, Careers, Wellbeing and more.

  • Questions and accepted answers

    Threaded answers with up and down votes; the person who asked marks the answer that helped.

  • Case discussions with photos

    Up to four photos per post, resized and stripped of location data, with a patient-privacy check before posting.

  • Polls

    Quick polls with secret votes: “What would you do?”

  • Profiles and stories

    Your own profile with a photo and bio, and 24-hour stories for events and moments.

  • Notifications

    Know when someone answers, replies, mentions you or follows you.

Community guidelines

Protect patients: never post anything that could identify a patient. Respect everyone, seniors, peers and other specialties alike. Cite sources. No advertising without the Founders’ Council’s approval. The Founders’ Council reviews every report.
